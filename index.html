Nobin · Oct 2024 to now

An assistant for elderly and disabled people that calls for help when something goes wrong. I am the sole engineer and built the device, the Android app and the server. Fall detection, the wake word, distress sounds and voice commands all run on a Raspberry Pi. The server only signals and relays: calls, push, and encrypted voice notes and clips it cannot read.
